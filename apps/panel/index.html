You & mods: !dj commands

Type in chat or put on a Stream Deck "chat message" button. Everything on this desk does the same thing.

!dj drop
The big drop moment
!dj hype
Hype finale: meter to full
!dj lights down / up
Lights down (lighters & phones) or back up
!dj colors ff3d81 19d3c5
Light colors as two hex color codes, or auto to follow the show
!dj motion sweep
How the lights move: fan, cross, wave, sweep, circle, ballyhoo, snap, tilt, or auto
!dj chase bounce
Which lights are on, on top of the movement: chase, bounce, center, random, pulse, none (all on), or auto
!dj look auto
Movement and chase both back to auto
!dj intensity 1.5
Brightness, 0 to 2
!dj glow 1
Light glow, 0 to 2
!dj speed 2
Light movement speed, 0.1 to 4
!dj lasers on
on, off or auto
!dj strobe on / off
Strobe on the beams and stage LEDs
!dj strobe 2
Strobe on at a speed: 0.5 (slow), 1 (every beat), 2 (fast), 4 (faster)
!dj blackout on / off
All lights off
!dj reset
Lights back to normal
!dj fire
Flame jets (!dj fire chase for a left-to-right chase)
!dj co2
CO2 geysers
!dj sparks
Spark fountains
!dj falls
Spark waterfall off the roof
!dj fireworks 10
Fireworks, up to 20
!dj pyro
Everything at once
!dj cam wide
auto, wide, sweep, over, close, back (stage)
!dj cut
Next camera shot now
!dj pace fast
How often auto cuts: slow, normal, fast
!dj zoom 1.5
Zoom, 0.7 to 2.4 (pan −13 to 13, height −3 to 5)
!dj frame
Reset zoom and framing
!dj shake
Camera shake
!dj focus name
Close-up on a viewer's Bibo
!dj screens heart
Side screens: auto, meter, stripes, checker, ripple, solid, off, heart, star, smile, note, bolt, fire, arrow, diamond
!dj screens text HELLO
Scrolling text on the side screens
!dj screens chat
Live Twitch chat on the side screens (commands, bots and links are left out; deleted messages come off)
!dj help / help off
Show or hide the commands card
!dj helpevery 10
Auto-show the card every N minutes (0 = never)
!dj jetpack name
Launch someone's jetpack (all = everyone)
!dj kick name
Remove someone's Bibo (bans and timeouts do this automatically)
!dj bpm 128
Set the tempo
!dj beat
Press on the 1 to line the lights up with the music
!dj nudge 20
Shift the beat by milliseconds (negative = earlier)
